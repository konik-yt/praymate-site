PRAYMATE

Delete your Praymate account

Praymate is a prayer times and alarm app for Android, provided by Uzair. This page explains how to delete your account and the data saved with it.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Praymate and sign in.
  2. Open Settings and go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Ask us to delete it

If you can't open the app, or you no longer have it installed, email us and we'll delete your account for you.

Email uzairbinadnan@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Praymate account. Send it from the Google address you use to sign in to Praymate, so we can find your account. We'll reply once it's deleted.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: your email address, name and profile picture from Google, and your account ID.
  • Your prayer history saved to your account: the date, prayer, confirmation time and response for each prayer.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Our hosting provider keeps short-term security logs, such as IP addresses and request times. They expire on their own.
  • Copies in our hosting provider's backups, if any, are removed when those backups expire.
  • Settings, alarms and location on your phone are never sent to us. Clear Praymate's data or uninstall the app to remove them.
