Kept only on your phone

Location. When you allow location access, Praymate reads your phone’s location to calculate prayer times and find your time zone. This happens on your phone. Your coordinates are saved on your phone so prayer times work offline. They are not sent to us.

Alarms and settings. Your alarm settings, the prayers you confirm, your calculation method and your other settings are saved on your phone.

App blocking. If you turn on app blocking, Praymate uses Android Accessibility access to see which app is on screen. It uses app and window identifiers only. It does not read screen text or controls and does not take screenshots. The apps you choose to block and blocker activity stay on your phone.

Audio pausing. If you allow Notification Access, Praymate pauses media playing from the apps you chose to block. It does not collect the content of your notifications.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this information in your phone’s backup to your Google account. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy.

Kept with your account

Account details. Your email address. If you sign up with a password, our sign-in provider stores it in a scrambled (hashed) form that we cannot read. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your email address and basic profile details, such as your name and profile picture.

Prayer history. For each prayer: the date, the prayer’s name, the time you confirmed it, and how quickly you responded or whether you missed it.

Technical records. Our hosting provider keeps short-term logs, such as IP addresses and request times, to run and secure the service. It also records when your account was created and when you last signed in.

How we use it

We use this information to sign you in, save and show your prayer history on any phone you sign in on, send password reset emails, and keep the service secure.

We do not sell your information. We do not show ads. Praymate does not include advertising or analytics tools.

Who else handles it

Supabase hosts our database and sign-in service and stores the information kept with your account.

Google handles sign-in if you choose to sign in with Google.

These providers may process information on servers outside Pakistan. We share information with others only if the law requires it.

Security

Information travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections. Our database only lets a signed-in account read and change its own prayer history.

How long we keep it

We keep your account details and prayer history until you delete your account. Information on your phone stays until you clear Praymate’s data or uninstall the app.

Deleting your account

Open Settings, go to Account and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and the prayer history saved to it from our database, and removes the copy of that history cached on your phone. Copies in our hosting provider’s backups, if any, are removed when those backups expire.

If you can’t open the app, email [Contact email] from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

Your choices

You can turn off location, notifications, Accessibility access or Notification Access in Android Settings at any time. Features that need that access stop working until you turn it back on.

You can ask us for a copy of the information kept with your account, or ask us to correct it, by emailing [Contact email].

Children

Praymate is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email [Contact email] and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.
