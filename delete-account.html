Praymate Privacy Policy

Delete your account

Last updated 19 November 2026

Praymate is an Android app for the five daily prayers, provided by Uzair. This page explains how to delete your Praymate account and what happens to the data saved with it.

On this page

  1. Delete it in the app
  2. Ask us to delete it
  3. What is deleted
  4. What is kept, and for how long
  5. Contact

Delete it in the app

Open Praymate and sign in. Open Settings, go to Account and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account and the prayer history saved to it are deleted straight away.

Ask us to delete it

If you can’t open the app or no longer have it installed, email uzairbinadnan@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Praymate account”. Send it from the Google address you use to sign in, so we can find your account. We will delete it and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your account: the email address, name and profile picture that Google shared with us, and your account ID.

Your prayer history saved to your account: for each prayer, the date, the prayer’s name, the time you confirmed it, and how quickly you responded or whether you missed it.

What is kept, and for how long

Our hosting provider keeps short-term logs, such as IP addresses and request times, to run and secure the service. They expire on their own.

Copies in our hosting provider’s backups, if any, are removed when those backups expire.

Your settings, alarms and location are saved only on your phone and are never sent to us. Clear Praymate’s data or uninstall the app to remove them.

Contact

Questions about deleting your account: uzairbinadnan@gmail.com.
