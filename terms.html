What Praymate does

Praymate calculates daily prayer times from your location, rings alarms at those times, keeps a record of the prayers you confirm, and can block selected apps until you confirm the current prayer.

Praymate is free. We may add, change or remove features at any time.

Prayer times

Prayer times are calculated on your phone from your location, the calculation method and the Asr setting you choose. They are estimates and can differ from the times your local mosque or religious authority uses.

Praymate is not a religious authority. When in doubt, follow the times set by your local mosque or scholar.

Alarms and app blocking

Alarms and app blocking rely on Android permissions, battery settings and limits set by your phone’s maker. Android can delay or stop an alarm, and some apps or playback cannot be blocked or paused. We cannot promise that every alarm will ring or every app will be blocked.

The app blocker is a self-help tool you turn on yourself. You can turn it off in Praymate or remove its access in Android Settings at any time.
