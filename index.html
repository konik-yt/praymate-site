Prayer times for where you are

Praymate works out Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha from your location, right on your phone. It needs no internet connection, and you can switch the calculation method to match your mosque.

These are today's real times, calculated in your browser.

    An alarm for every prayer

    Turn each prayer's alarm on or off, set it to ring at the prayer time or a few minutes after, and give each one its own sound. Alarms keep ringing when the app is closed and come back after your phone restarts.

    Answer with one tap

    When the alarm rings, tap I'm praying. Not ready yet? Tap Delay and choose how many minutes to wait, from 1 to 120, and Praymate reminds you again.

    See how you're doing

    Every answer is saved, first on your phone and then to your account. The history grid colors each prayer by how many reminders it took, so the prayers you missed stand out.

    A sky that follows your day

    The picture changes with the time: dawn before sunrise, day until Asr, evening until Isha, and night after that. The Islamic date moves to the next day at sunset.

    Pick a time of day to see this page change with it.

    This can be your persistent reminder, right in your pocket.
